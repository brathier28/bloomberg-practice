⏺ Here are the key rules, open questions, and assumptions from the spec:

  ---

  Participant IDs
  - Zero-padded two-digit sequence: "00", "01", ... "10", etc.
  - Question (already noted): What happens past "99"? The spec says "any number
    of participants" but IDs are two-digit. Does it roll to three digits? Raise
    an error? This is undefined.
  - IDs are assigned by join order — they appear to be deterministic and
    sequential, not random.

  Display Names
  - Duplicate names are allowed. Each is a distinct participant with their own
    share.
  - Assumption: The spec says "not more than three" in your personal notes —
    unclear what this refers to (words? characters?). The spec body doesn't
    state a name length limit, so this may be something to clarify.

  Expenses
  - Snapshot of participants at the moment record_expense is called — latecomers
    are excluded from past expenses.
  - total_cents is always an integer (no fractional cents ever).
  - Expense has its own id field — the spec doesn't say how it's generated.
    Assumption: probably sequential like participants.

  Splitting (the core logic)
  - Floor division: base = total_cents // n
  - Remainder: r = total_cents % n — exactly r participants get one extra cent.
  - Top-up assignment is strictly by join order (earliest joiners first).
  - Zero remainder → every participant gets exactly base cents.
  - Edge case: What if total_cents = 0? Valid — every participant gets 0.
  - Edge case: Single participant gets 100% of the total (no remainder possible
    since n=1).

  Lifecycle
  - Two distinct SplitTabError cases: recording on a closed tab, and
    double-closing.
  - No mention of re-opening a tab — assumption: once closed, permanently
    closed.
  - No restriction on zero participants when recording an expense — question:
    can you call record_expense before anyone has joined? The spec doesn't
    explicitly forbid it, which would mean n=0 and a division-by-zero.

  count_payers and plan_splits
  - These are standalone functions, not methods on SplitTab, so they operate on
    an Expense object after the fact.
  - plan_splits returns a SplitPlan with splits (list of Split) and
    total_distributed — assumption: total_distributed should always equal
    expense.total_cents.
